## Suggested submission description

SecondServe is an AI-assisted surplus-food rescue prototype. Providers can use an explicitly consented Muse Spark photo draft, review their remaining stock, and publish a discounted offer. Nearby users can inspect the provider's sample storefront, compare pickup details, and place a simulated order. A separate rescue workflow checks partner constraints, recovers from cancellations, and counts food only after pickup is verified. All businesses, inventory, partners, locations, and orders are demo data; checkout does not charge and no external dispatch occurs.
