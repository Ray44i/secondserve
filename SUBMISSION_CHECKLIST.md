## Suggested submission description

SecondServe is an AI-assisted food-rescue coordination prototype. It helps a coordinator turn a surplus report into an explainable, constraint-checked recipient shortlist, recover when a partner cancels, and record impact only after a pickup quantity is verified. Meta Muse Spark creates structured drafts from donor notes, can suggest visible food identity from an explicitly consented photo, and can explain deterministic match results for human review. All businesses, listings, partners, locations, and offers in the demo are simulated; no real food-safety determination or external dispatch is performed.
