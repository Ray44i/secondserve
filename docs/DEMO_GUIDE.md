# Three-minute SecondServe demo guide

## Story

A bakery has 20 croissants and a short pickup window. The first partner accepts and then cannot collect. SecondServe releases the reservation, re-evaluates remaining eligible partners, and offers the batch to a replacement. The recipient reports a pickup; the coordinator verifies the actual number, and only then does the impact ledger update.

## Run of show

- 0:00–0:20 — State the problem and disclose that all partner records are demo fixtures.
- 0:20–0:50 — Coordinator loads recovery and runs matching; show hard filters and score rationale.
- 0:50–1:20 — Dispatch, switch to Charity A, accept, then cancel.
- 1:20–1:50 — Return to coordinator; show released capacity, recovery event, and replacement offer.
- 1:50–2:20 — Community Kitchen B accepts and reports pickup; show reported quantity is not yet counted.
- 2:20–2:40 — Coordinator verifies quantity; show verified impact grouped by unit.
- 2:40–3:00 — Show the review scenario or optional Muse Spark intake draft, and state its limits.

## Keep it honest

Do not imply an external notification was sent, that recipients are real, that demo distance is live mapping, that model output certifies food, or that the prototype is production-secure. Rehearse role switching and scenario reset before recording.
